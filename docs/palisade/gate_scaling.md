## 3. What this does to the $10^4$-job ensemble claim

The manuscript computes $10^4$ jobs $\times$ the marginal G5 cost and reports about 1.3 s at the fast tier. Measured, one process sustains **6,395 G5 checks/s**, so $10^4$ submissions take **1.56 s** of mediation --- the extrapolation was sound for a single process, which is worth stating as a confirmed result rather than an assumed one.

Across 10 cores the same campaign takes **0.33 s** at 30,453/s. Either way the fast tier is not the bottleneck in an ensemble campaign: at these rates mediation is dominated by the scheduler's own admission path and by queue wait, which is the comparison a facility cares about. The slow tier is the configuration ruled out for this workload, and that conclusion is unchanged.
